## Limitations carried forward

- Project-user evidence originates from one business-authored BRD rather than primary user research.
- The personas are provisional, overlapping need-based archetypes; prevalence and segment stability remain unknown.
- Pain frequency, severity, and relative priority are not supported by behavioural or representative data.
- Fulfillment, activation, and automated-recommendation concerns retain Weak project evidence and require validation.
- Pricing comprehension, support-channel preference, trade-in understanding, and the usefulness of preference-based guidance remain open research questions.
- Much of the external evidence is U.S.-specific, while the target market is unspecified.
- Public competitor documentation describes intended processes rather than authenticated account-specific usability.
- Community evidence is anecdotal and non-representative.
- The concept intentionally excludes explainable automated recommendations from initial scope because that Helpful opportunity has Weak evidence.
